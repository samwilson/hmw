**Gordon Wilson** was born on 21 June 1906.
Married {link|people/helen-nelson}.
Married {link|people/jessie-bowden} in 1929 in Perth.

Posted to somewhere in New South Wales (near a good bathing beach) in October 1943, from Melbourne.{ref|edith1943}

Died aged 56, on 9 February 1963{ref|death} and buried at Karrakatta with his parents.{ref|grave}
Grave stone reads:

{{{quotation
In loving memory of our beloved husband & father James Herbert Wilson
born 5th Feb. 1872, died 1st June 1942.
Also our beloved mother Edith Olive Wilson born 8th Sept. 1892, died 18th Aug, 1966.
Frederick Gordon Wilson 1906–1963
}}}
