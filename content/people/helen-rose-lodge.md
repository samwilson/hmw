**Helen Rose Lodge** was born on 3 April 1888 in Grass Valley (near Northam), Western Australia{ref|FamilyNotices} 
to {link|people/thomas-soutter-lodge} and {link|people/sarah-constance-leake}.{ref|BMD}

She worked at {wdl|Q133094135|Pinjarra Park}{ref|item22} at some point,
and later moved to Cossack to work as a ladies' maid to Aubrey Hall's mother {link|people/hannah-boyd-lazenby|Hannah}.

She married{ref|HAHmarriage} {link|people/harold-aubrey-hall} on 24 November 1910{ref|HallLodgeWedding} when she was 23.
They had three daughters: Constance (1912), Margaret (1915), and Joan (1919).
