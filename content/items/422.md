Stirling House is the RWAHS's old premises in Broadway, Nedlands.

## Transcription

Copy of letter from H.A. Hall in Stirling
House records regarding
site of graves of the members of Frank
Gregory's N.W. Exploration Party 18/1/62

{{{page-break}}}

P.S. Please pass
on to {?|Joan} {?}

36 Congdon St\
Swanbourne\
6 Aug /56

Dear Mr {?|Chali}

Apologies for
having an absent minded
{?|moment}, herewith I would amend.

Regarding the printed
proceedings of the Society, do
members {?|secure} these free
or do we copy to you and
pay for them?

{{{page-break}}}

My wife & I have been members for many
years but we do not appear to secure
any thing except notices of important
meetings & fees due.

I am nomadic but Mrs Hall's a/d has
always been available.

I would very much like to posses a
{?|complete} set of the Society's Proceedings.
Can you put me in the way of {?|securing}
{?|these} & if so at what cost?

Would it be of interest to the H.S. to
record the sites of the graves of the
members of Frank Gregory's North West
Exploration Party 1861/2? If so I
would mention that my father
W.S. Hall's grant is in the Cemetery
of the {?} of Cossack &
[Jas Turner](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Turner-19424) who was Second in

{{{page-break}}}

Command is {?|also interred}.

Jas Turner was of the
Augusta {?|pioneer} family,
my father landed in W.A.
July 1830.

[Pemberton Walcott](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Walcott-514) also
a member of Gregory's
Party his in Broome
Cemetery & has or had
a tombstone.

My father established Andover
Station 9 m S of Roebourne for
John Wellard of Lowlands,
Serpentine in 1863. Eight months
prior to John Withnell's arrival.

Truly yours\
H.A. Hall.

{{{page-break}}}

Library Board of Western Australia\
Memorandum Ref. MB\
To Mrs H. Wilson\
Re attached.

Returned herewith. We
have typed a copy.

I am ashamed to see
that you left us this on
13/7/74 and it has still
not been returned.

My apologies and thanks\
Margaret Medcalf\
14/10/74
