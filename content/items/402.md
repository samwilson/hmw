## Transcription

141, Orron Road,\
Elsternwick S4.\
Vic.

Sunday 29th August, 1954.

Darling Dad,

Enclosed the snaps of
Mother. She looks so well and happy.
I think she will be in Colombo
to-morrow. Do wish I could be
there to welcome her when she gets
home, but I will try to ring that
night.

We are settling in the flat
very happily and have acquired some
furniture for the bedroom, about
which I will tell in the family
letter.

Went to hear {{wdl|Q177248|Dame Sybil
Thorndyke} last night — English as

{{{page-break}}}

II

last night — a carbon copy of a letter
to Mother on an aerogramme {?|soon}.
Hope it is decipherable. I hope to
get to some serious letter writing
on Sunday.

Meantime, darling, do please
take great care of yourself, and I
hope the shearing will not be too
strenuous. Let the lad do the
worrying, and you sleep sound
o'nights.

Much love to my dear, dear
Dad

from\
his affectionate daughter,\
Joanee.
