## Transcription

Premier's Office,\
Perth.

30th April, 1908.

No 189

Your Worship—

{u|Empire Day, 1908.}

I desire to invite your co-operation in connection
with the Celebration, which it is hoped will take place
annually in your town, proposed to be held on the above day.

For your information I may state that I am
arranging for Wednesday, 27th May, as the day on which these
Celebrations should take place, as it is expected that {wdl|Q1452799|His
Excellency the Governor} will return to the State on the 26th
prox.

The Education Department are again taking up the
matter and I trust you will see your way clear to act in a
similar manner to the last occasion, when a gathering of the
children was one of the principal features of the Celebrations,
and the opportunity was taken to impress upon them the
interests that bind the Empire together, the desirability of
preserving it intact and of strengthening the bonds of unity,
and the duty that devolves upon them of endeavoring the become
worthy holders of so great a trust.

I have the honor to be,\
Your Worship's obedient Servant,\
{wdl|Q5722206|H Gregory}\
Acting Premier

His Worship\
The Mayor of Cossack
