## Transcription

Cossack W.A.\
23 May 1908

The {?|Honourable}\
The Acting Premier\
Perth W.A.

Sir

Empire Day. We are {?|planning}
with {?|the launch} on Monday week in our
observances, the programme for which
includes addresses & exercises for the
children, {?|suitable} to the occasion.
I am whole-heartedly with those who would
lead the {?|nation} of tomorrow to a real appreciation
of the Imperial heritage to
which they have been born & {?}
my small endeavours will always be to that {?|part}.

Yours very truly

Aubrey Hall
