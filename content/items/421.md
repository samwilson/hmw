## Transcription

50th Vic., No. 25.—Schedule.

AGREEMENT.

This Agreement, made the 9th day of July
1894 between WS Hall
of Cossack and Carnmarra alias Whalebone
as aboriginal native, witnesseth:—

1\. The said Whalebone agrees
to serve WS Hall
as general servant.

2\. Such service to last until July 9th 1895.

3\. Whalebone is to begin
work on the 9th day of July 1894.

4\. WS Hall is to supply
Whalebone during the said
service with medicines and medical attendance when practicable and
necessary, unless the illness of Whalebone
be caused by his own improper act or default.
