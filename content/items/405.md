## Transcription

2, Northfield Crescent,\
Edinburgh 8.\
2nd May. 1946.

Dear Margaret,

Two West Australians & a Woman
(only paper ones, alas!) arrived this morning. The
papers come very regularly now and I do enjoy
looking at them. From the long lists of Sits. Vac.
I think I shall be able to get a job all
right. Was interested in the job, or rather the rate,
for a railway porter "17/6 per day for first year…
plus district allowance … liberal sick leave permission…",
leave three weeks after 12 months service. Our
adult Grade 2 Porters start at 85/- per week of 48 hrs
(this includes War Bonus of 28/-) do not get any
pay when off sick, & only this year have been
granted two weeks annual leave with pay. When
I started on the railway in 1936 a porters' weekly
wage was 40/- per week. Don't ask me how they
kept home & families! But I mustn't talk shop.

There is a nasty little doubt in my mind
regarding the acknowledgement & thanks for that very
lovely "birthday" gift. Surely I thanks you before!
