## Transcription

Station from: Perth\
No. of Words: 44 \
Check: \
Time Lodged: 10a \
Date Lodged:

Reply Paid Ernest Hall Roebourne

Have wired A B Flinders unless you have
better offer than twenty pounds I accept if
nineteen pounds transmitted through bank
for my Credit Union Bank Perth one pound
for you

J.H. Ellory\
Stirling St Perth
