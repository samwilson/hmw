## Transcription

You were only attached \
For a very short time\
And it's not very easy\
To write you a rhyme.\
But I liked knowing you\
And I still hear your voice\
And of my three room mates\
You were the most choice\
One was too ordinary\
With no personality.\
And one is so rude,\
And has no versatility\
You laughed at my nonsense\
And made me at home\
And now that you've gone\
I feel all alone\
I rush round the barracks\
And talk to the cooks\
I fling open windows\
And get dirty looks\
But there's no one to play with\
And I hate Training Group\
Inspecting the Grease Traps\
And tasting the soup\
The cat had had kittens\
I had to drown five\
I left only one of them save alive.
